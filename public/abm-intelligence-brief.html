Methodology

// Three objections this brief expects, addressed on the record
// Sample Selection Bias

Public reviewers skew toward dissatisfied customers. We treat this as a feature: friction is counted only in 1-2★ reviews, which deliberately excludes positive mentions. The 250 friction mentions that survive this gate are not a population-representative survey. They are an early-warning indicator of where billing and cancellation friction is loud enough to reach a public channel.

// 1 Friction Mention ≈ 1 Lost Member (Directional)

The 1:1 friction-to-churn assumption is directional, not actuarial. The Revenue Impact Estimator publishes the full sensitivity range (30% to 100% conversion) and leads with the 60% midpoint. Anyone uncomfortable with the ceiling can read the floor and still find the recoverable AR material.

// How We Identified Your Operators

The operator base was mapped from the public Ahrefs backlink graph of [platform-domain.com]: businesses that link back to the platform (booking widgets, "powered by" badges, partner pages) are surfaced as candidates, then filtered against Google Business results to confirm they are live operators. No proprietary platform data was used.
