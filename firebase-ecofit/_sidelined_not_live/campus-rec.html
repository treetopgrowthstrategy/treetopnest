Your floor plan was set at construction. Your students were not.

Gate counts tell you who came in. They cannot tell you what got used. Strength demand keeps climbing while rows of cardio sit idle, functional zones overflow at peak, and the recovery space everyone asked for goes unmeasured. What Ecofit measures

Six answers every rec director gets asked for.

Continuous measurement, reported in plain terms your team, your committees, and your administration can act on.

Utilization by machine and zone

See which equipment earns its floor space and which is furniture, from individual treadmills to the whole free-weight area.

Semester rhythm and peak periods

Syllabus-week rush, midterm dips, finals, summer sessions. Know your real peaks by hour and by term, and plan staffing and hours around them.

Strength vs. cardio demand trends

Track how training preferences shift across cohorts, so the cardio-heavy mix of a decade ago evolves into the floor today's students want.

Maintenance and lifecycle signals

Usage-based wear and automated error codes flag what needs service, so equipment is up before peak weeks and replaced on evidence, not age alone.

Administration-ready reporting

Usage per student, cost per use, and participation trends in reports built for the people who approve budgets and student fees.

Renovation and purchase planning

Walk into your next capital project knowing exactly what your population uses, instead of buying the same mix you bought last time.
