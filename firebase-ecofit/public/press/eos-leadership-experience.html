ecofit Joins EōS Fitness at its 2026 EōS Experience in Las Vegas, July 13-15, 2026

As one of the country's fastest-growing operators scales, ecofit's total club intelligence gives EōS a real-time read on how every club performs and how every machine holds up.

Victoria, BC, July 6, 2026

ecofit, the equipment intelligence platform for fitness operators, will join EōS Fitness at its EōS Experience on July 13 to 15, 2026. The event brings EōS leaders together as the brand continues one of the most aggressive expansion runs in the industry.

EōS works with ecofit to bring a best-in-class understanding of how its clubs actually operate. As EōS continues to open new locations, it becomes essential to expand with the absolute best understanding of two things: how the equipment on every floor is holding up, and how members are really using each club. ecofit measures both, in real time, across every brand and across every location.

For a growing operator, that visibility compounds. Every new club is a chance to get the equipment mix right the first time, to protect uptime before a member ever notices a problem, and to make the next purchasing decision on data instead of instinct. Standardizing that intelligence across a portfolio the size of EōS turns a hundred separate floors into one clear picture.

"EōS does not guess, and that is exactly why this partnership works. As they open club after club, they want to know that every piece of equipment is earning its place and that every floor is serving members the way it should. We give them that answer in real time. Being part of the EōS Experience means being in the room with the people making those calls."

Dave Johnson, Co-Founder and CEO, ecofit
